Cat Tower Cat strategy for Android

Your cats want every box in the house. Draw a line from one of your boxes to another and your cats march off to take it, before the rival cats get there. A quick strategy game for Android, played with a single finger.

How it plays

  • Draw a line from your box to another: your cats walk along it and, when they arrive, take the box if they outnumber the cats inside.
  • Boxes grow on their own and level up: the bigger they get, the more lines they can send at once.
  • Cut your lines with the scissors to change plans in the middle of a match.
  • 48 levels against one, two or three rival colonies, and two tutorials to learn the game in a minute.
  • 1 vs 1 against a friend with a code, and ranked 1 vs 1 with trophies and a world leaderboard.
  • Collect cat cards to make your cats stronger, more agile and faster. Free: ads only if you choose to watch one for a reward.
